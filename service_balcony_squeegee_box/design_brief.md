# Service balcony squeegee box

External width 420 mm, depth 245 mm, front height 110 mm, back height
170 mm. Open top with sloping side rims, based on supplied sketch.
Coordinates: X width, Y depth (front negative), bottom Z=0.
Assumed wall 3 mm, floor 6 mm, outer plan corners R9 / inner R6,
inside floor R3, exposed rim and bottom edges R0.8.
Centered uppercase SQUEEGEE is recessed 1.2 mm into the front wall,
leaving 1.8 mm wall behind the letters (not a through-cut).

Split at X=0 with 0.10 mm seam. Left male/right female. Adapted from
../tool_boxes/drawer_box_geometry.py revision 3: same 4/7.6 mm trapezoid
neck/head, 7 mm reach, 0.10 mm socket relief, 45 degree supported male
underside and matching seated socket; 8 mm wide, 3 mm reach floor tabs
with 0.08 mm relief. Rail lengths adapted to front/back heights.
No magnets. Rounded body corners; precision mating edges retain their
original connector profile. Lower left half vertically into right half.
All source, STEP, printable STL, reference, checks and renders live here.
Validation: positive closed solids; overall bounds; half print envelopes;
non-overlap seated and during upward motion; downward stop; snapshots.
