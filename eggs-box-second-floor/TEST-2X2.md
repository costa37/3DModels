## Check it

1. On a table, put four representative large eggs in all four bowls. Check both rows and columns: eggs should sit securely without forcing them together. Inspect contact surfaces for strings and sharp spots.
2. Fit the leg until the shoulder touches the underside. In the loaded box, position the sample over the corresponding right-hand two columns and center/outer two rows. The outside long edge is the edge nearest the socket. Keep the leg on its intended floor land; do not slide it to an arbitrary position to make it fit.
3. Support the other edges by hand and keep the tray level at the leg's shoulder height. The underside should be 52.1 mm above the foot's support plane. A single leg does NOT make the sample a stable stand. Do not leave it loaded unsupported or press down on eggs below.
4. Check leg-to-egg side clearance and tray underside clearance above the lower eggs. Aim for at least 5 mm empty clearance, allowing further margin for full-tray flex. Mirror the test position to check other legs if useful; this crop alone does not simultaneously test all four feet.

Pass means your tested eggs fit the 2×2 arrangement and the checked region clears below. Full-tray rocking, loaded sag, complete footprint and finger access still require the full tray. No load-strength claim follows from this sample.
